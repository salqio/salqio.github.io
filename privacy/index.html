Privacy

Salq is a weekly money desk for companies (Dutch BV, ZZP, and foreign customers who hire desks). We are software, not a bank. This page is the public notice for salq.io — including the URL Stripe and other processors can check.

Who we are

The controller for this website is Salq. Contact: hello@salq.io. We do not hold your money, collect iDEAL for your customers, or pay your vendors.

Waitlist

The public site may ask for a work email so we can tell you when a spot opens. That address is for that message. It is not a customer account and not a mailing list for other products.

Bank files and invoices

When you later use the desk, invoices and uploaded bank exports belong to your company tenant. They are for matching payments. We do not sell bank data. We do not scrape your bank by logging in.

Payments

Hiring a desk by the month is not on this website yet. When card payments start, Salq stays the merchant and a payment processor (Stripe) will handle cards. Card numbers are not collected on these public pages. There are no payment keys on this site.

Not advice

Leak flags and “safe to pay” are not financial, tax, or credit advice. See Not financial advice.

What we do not do

We do not sell waitlist addresses. We do not need a live money feed to explain the office on this site. Public pages do not show tenant invoices or matches.
